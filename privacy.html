Privacy Policy

Guilty Tape Uploader — last updated 13 November 2026.

Guilty Tape Uploader is a personal-use application operated by its owner to upload documentary videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (Guilty Tape and Fallow Years) using the YouTube Data API. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app through Google's OAuth consent screen, the app receives permission to:

  • Upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels (youtube.upload scope).
  • Read the owner's channel and video status to confirm uploads (youtube.readonly scope).

The app accesses only the owner's own Google account and YouTube channels. It does not access any other person's account, videos, or data.

What data the app stores

The app stores only what is needed to perform uploads on the owner's behalf:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, held in the secure credential store of the automation platform the owner uses to run the app. Tokens are never written to files, logs, or chat messages.
  • Video metadata the owner supplies for each upload (title, description, tags, thumbnail, scheduled publish time).

What the app does with data

Data is used solely to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels and to confirm each upload's status. The app does not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose any data to any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking is used.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens remain stored until the owner revokes the app's access, at which point they stop working immediately. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account settings, under Security → Third-party access. Video metadata is kept only as part of the owner's own production records.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not made for children.
