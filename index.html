Guilty Tape Uploader

A personal tool for publishing documentary videos to YouTube.

Guilty Tape Uploader is a small, personal-use application that uploads finished documentary videos to the YouTube channels run by its owner, using the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service and has no other users.

The channels it serves:

  • Guilty Tape — true crime documentaries, built from public records, released police footage, and court testimony.
  • Fallow Years — history documentaries about the stories history left behind.

What the app does:

  • Uploads video files to the owner's own YouTube channels, with the titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and scheduled publish times the owner chooses.
  • Reads back the upload status to confirm each video and its scheduled publish time.

What the app does not do:

  • It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • It does not access any Google account other than the owner's.
  • It does not modify, delete, or manage any videos beyond uploading new ones.
